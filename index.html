Bio

About

KOVA is a Chicago producer and DJ working between melodic house and live electronics. The sets are built around long, patient builds and a lot of low end, played from a rig that keeps enough unfixed to respond to the room. Since 2023 there have been two EPs, a monthly residency, and support slots across the Midwest. The 2026 record is being written in a basement in Pilsen and will be finished when it is finished.
